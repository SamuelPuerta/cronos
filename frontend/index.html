Cronos

a dead letter office, of sorts

The old stories say Cronos ruled over time itself — and that he swallowed whatever wasn't looked after in time. This is a much smaller version of that idea: a quiet clerk who holds onto a letter, checks in on you now and then, and only sends it along if you go quiet for longer than you said you would.

Write it here, say who it's for, and tell Cronos how long to wait between one sign of life and the next. Keep checking in from your own private slip, and nothing happens. Stop, and Cronos assumes the worst.

There's no dashboard, no account, nothing to lose track of. Just the link you get after writing — treat it like a spare key, not a bookmark. — keep it somewhere you'll remember
